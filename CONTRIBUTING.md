# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Supplier Assurance Copilot.

This repository is published primarily as a portfolio and reference implementation. External
issues and pull requests are not currently being accepted.

You may read, clone, run, and adapt the project subject to the terms of the MIT License. If you
adapt it for your own use, keep credentials, private endpoints, supplier documents, proprietary
policies, and generated review outputs containing sensitive information out of version control.
